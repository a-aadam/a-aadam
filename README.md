## 🛡️ About me

I'm a **Security Engineer** with a blue-team background. I've worked in **24/7 Security Operations Centres** at **Accenture**, **BDO** and **EASI**, and in my latest role I **built a new local SOC team from the ground up**.

I work across the whole detection lifecycle: I tune SIEM rules to cut noise, investigate incidents end to end, write the playbooks and automation that let analysts scale, and train the people who run them.

- 🏰 **Founder of [Purple Bastion](https://purplebastion.com):** Cybersecurity-as-a-Service for SMEs and growing businesses (managed detection, incident response and security governance at a price that fits)
- **Learning:** AI Security and overnance (ISO/IEC 42001), digital forensics, and a Cyber Security Management Diploma (IATA) focused on aviation security
- **Let's talk** if your business needs a SOC it can't build in-house → [purplebastion.com](https://purplebastion.com)
